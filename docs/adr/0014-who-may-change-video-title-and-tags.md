# Who may change a Video’s title and Tags

An uploaded Video’s title and Tags may be changed by its Video owner, and on a Custom channel also by the Channel owner and Managers. An indexed Folder Video’s title may be changed only by an Admin; any signed-in Person may attach or remove its Tags. Frozen Home channel Videos keep the title and Tags they have. Title is catalogue-only: the slug and the file on the Media share stay. Putting title with the visibility set keeps a shared Custom channel tidy; Admin-only Folder titles keep archive names with the operator; household Folder Tags exist because Folder Videos start untagged and Tags are labels the house invents. The same Admin-only right for Folder title and Tags would leave the archive unlabeled; any signed-in Person renaming Folder Videos would mix household labeling with operator control of the index. An Admin tidy-right on Frozen Home would fill the missing Video owner by default.
