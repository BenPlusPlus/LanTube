# Who may delete a Video

An uploaded Video may be deleted by its Video owner, and on a Custom channel also by the Channel owner and Managers. An indexed Folder Video may be dropped from the catalogue only by an Admin, and the file on the Media share stays. Admin and Parent do not get a blanket delete right. Restricting delete to the Video owner alone would leave a shared channel impossible to tidy; letting Admin or Parent delete anything would mix operator/parent sight with library control.
