# Playlist membership does not grant Video access

A Playlist’s visibility is independent of the Videos in it. Sharing a private Playlist with someone does not let them see a private Video they were not already allowed to see; that Video is simply missing from their view of the Playlist. The other path is “playlist access implies video access,” which is convenient and would leak Tomás’s private clip because it sat on Mia’s playlist. Each Video keeps its own Audience, Channel owner, Managers, and Parent rules.
