# Deleting a Custom channel

The Channel owner may delete a Custom channel from the edit form. An Admin may delete a Custom channel, or a Frozen Home, from the channel page. That delete removes every Video on it, including private Videos on a Frozen Home, following the bytes rule (uploads leave the Media share). Deleting a Frozen Home also frees its slug. Managers and Parent cannot delete the channel from those roles. A live Home channel is not deleted; it freezes when the Person is removed. Refusing to delete a channel that still has Videos was rejected for this cut: one confirm, then the channel and its Videos go.
