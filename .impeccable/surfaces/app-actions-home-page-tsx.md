# Surface brief: LanTube home (/)

Scope: the household home feed, the first page anyone on the LAN sees. Mode: Operate (the visitor's task is getting to a video they want).
Audience/job: anyone in the house, on phone, laptop, tablet or TV, equally. Balanced job: resume what you started, see what's new in the house, pick a person's channel.
Constraints: no YouTube colour scheme; fonts and assets self-hosted (watch path never needs the WAN); identity mechanism undecided, so no profile switcher or sign-in invented.
Chosen: the category standard (canon), benchmark craft = YouTube, Rumble. Sketch: .impeccable/mocks/decision/canon.html.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A video-site home that anyone who has used YouTube or Rumble trusts instantly, executed at their craft level, refusing YouTube's red/black identity and refusing novelty layouts.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite dark ground (#0f1012), raised #222428 surfaces, #f1f1f1 text, one blue accent (#3d8bff) for primary action, selection, focus and watch progress only. Inter, one family. 12px thumbnail radii, pill chips, initial-letter avatars per person, a house-with-play-triangle mark, outlined 24px stroke icons.

STORY: The visitor sees their household's videos immediately, understands these are the house's own channels, resumes or picks something in one click, and knows uploads come from people and folders on this network.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 56px top bar (menu, mark+LanTube, centred 560px pill search, blue Upload). 240px left nav (Home/Channels/Playlists/History, then household channels, then indexed folders). Main: topic chips row, then "Continue watching" row with blue progress bars, then the "New in the house" 4-column grid. Primary action = click a thumbnail.

FORM: Category standard (canon card), outside the ordered list; seed key 488a730a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
